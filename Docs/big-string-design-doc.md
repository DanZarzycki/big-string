# Big String Design Document

## Setting
* Different clans of dwarves have lived through to the modern day.
* Each clan of dwarves has a mastery of a different tool, passed down by generations.
* Treasure is buried deep within sink holes, and is very valuable.
* But, each hoard of treasure is cursed in some way.
* Sink holes open, and dwarves explore to find buried treasure, grab it, and get out.
* Dwarves travel in caravans to different sink holes

## Story
* The Big String clan has mastered the art of string building and passed it down for generations.
* You play Belo, a dwarf from the Big String clan, using the big string to look for treasure.

### Curses
When you dig up treasure, a random curse is assigned. Curse should do the following:
* Make the ascent different than descent in some way.
* Make the ascent challenging.
* Force the player up with a time limit.

Curses could include:
* Curse of Water: Water level rises
* Curse of Earth: Earth blocks appear to make ascension more difficult.
* Curse of Wind: Wind blows the character.
* Curse of Lava: Lava rises as you go, frying you if you touch it. Spouts of lava shoot up in different areas, dealing damage on a hit and burning rope if touched. 
* Curse of Spirits: Ghost chases you up the tunnel. More ghosts spawn as you go.
* Curse of Aging: The string becomes frail and your character will die of old age on a timer.

#### Goals of mechanic
* Make ascension of the sink hole different and interesting.
* Provide random elements to navigating each excursion

### Randomization of elements
Randomize many elements, including but not limited to:
* Environment of the sinkhole.
* Obstacles in the sinkhole.
* Treasure at the bottom.
* Treasure's curse.

#### Goals of mechanic
* Provide unique experience and challenges each run
* Give replay value to each play session and playing a run

## Gameplay loop
1. Descend a sink hole
1. Collect treasure
1. Trigger curse
1. Ascend sink hole with curse active
1. Use treasure to buy help for next run, upgrade the caravan, and support clan for the future
